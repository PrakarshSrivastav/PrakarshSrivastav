## 👋 About Me

I'm a backend engineer who enjoys turning complex problems into clean, reliable systems. My focus is **distributed backend architecture** — services that scale, stay fast under load, and are boring in the best way (i.e. they just work). Lately I've been pulling AI into the stack: retrieval-augmented generation, vector search, and low-latency inference pipelines.

- 🔭 Currently building **enterprise-grade backend services** with Spring Boot & Kotlin
- ☁️ Deploying **cloud-native microservices** on AWS & Azure with Docker/Kubernetes and full CI/CD
- 🧠 Applying **PyTorch** to RAG pipelines and vector databases
- 🌱 Actively contributing to and maintaining **open-source** projects
- 💬 Always up for a conversation about system design, cloud architecture, or ML infra
